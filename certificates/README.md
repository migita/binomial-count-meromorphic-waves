# Computational certificates

The paper proves that all normalized rational-exponential pairs are distinct (the matching scheme `X_p` is reduced)
whenever `2p-1` is prime. At the composite indices below `2p-1 = 25` it relies on exact computations:

| `p` | index `2p-1` | pairs | certificate |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 5 | 9 | 126 | Gröbner basis over `Q`, in the arXiv package: `paper/anc/p5_count.sing` |
| 8 | 15 | 6435 | finite-field Gröbner basis and Jacobian, `p8/` |
| 11 | 21 | 352716 | local certificates over the 19-adic numbers, `p11/` |

Together with the prime-index theorem this gives distinctness for every `2 <= p <= 12`. The first open case is `p = 13`.

## `p8/` — finite field and Hensel lifting

Over `F_32003` the matching ideal has quotient dimension 6435 and generates the unit ideal together with its Jacobian
determinant. Every geometric point of the reduction is therefore simple. Each lifts uniquely to a simple point in characteristic
zero by the multivariable Hensel lemma (as in Step 4 of the prime-index theorem), the lifts are distinct, and they are all the
points because the characteristic-zero length is 6435 (rigidity at degree 7). No flatness argument is needed.

Two independent runs of Singular:

- `run1_p8_q32003.sing`, `run1_p8_q32003.out`: 2 September 2026 (about 1490 s). The transcript also shows that the leading
  (continuous-convolution) system is zero-dimensional modulo 32003 (`DIM0 0`, `VDIM0 6435`).
- `full_p8_q32003_dp_slimgb.*`, `generate_systems.py`, `run_singular.py`: 17 September 2026, input reconstructed independently
  from the finite sums that define `S_A`; `slimgb`, Jacobian determinant reduced in the quotient ring (386 s).
  The 131 MB Gröbner basis produced by this run is not stored; it is regenerated by the script.

## `p11/` — archived certificate

`codex_enumeration_paper_certificate_record.tar.gz` (11 958 177 bytes, SHA-256
`4c6f1e31260032a38ae32883e2ec6fab62222925930b6fa957ce12ac0d890a69`) is the complete record of the computation of
2 September 2026: 270 files with a manifest. The method works at the prime 19 = 2p-3. Modulo 19 the closure polynomial factors
as `u (x^19 - x) S_A`, so the points of the reduction are explicit, and the 352716 characteristic-zero points are obtained as

| points of the reduction modulo 19 | characteristic-zero points certified |
| --- | ---: |
| simple, rational over `F_19` (Hensel) | 119781 |
| simple over `F_361` (Hensel) | 87372 |
| singular of corank one (Newton-polygon certificate) | 97971 |
| singular of corank two (Newton–Kantorovich certificates in local fields) | 47592 |
| total = length of `X_11` | 352716 |

- `fable_p11_certificate_final.md`: the final accounting; `fable_local_certificates_justification.md`: the lemmas behind the two
  kinds of local certificate.
- `recovered_simplicity_note.md`, `p11_archive_integrity.json`, `p11_record_validation.json`, `p11_high_precision_replay.json`,
  `validate_p11_record.py`: validation of the archive on 17 September 2026 — all 270 manifest entries verified; the ten rational
  matching equations reconstructed independently and matched to the archived ones; the corank-one inventory (47801 residue labels,
  each once, total 97971) and the merged corank-two audit (11592 labels, each once, total 47592) checked; the four final
  high-precision records replayed (16 simple points, no failure).

This is an archived computation with its verification record. The whole computation was not rerun in full on 17 September.
The producer scripts inside the archive hard-code working paths; see the README inside the archive before replaying them.

## Pure subfamilies

The finite-field certificates for the purely dispersive and purely dissipative subfamilies (`p <= 14`) are small and are part of
the arXiv package: `paper/anc/symmetry_checks/modular/`.

## `rigidity_572/` — counts with multiplicity through order 573

[rigidity_572/README.md](rigidity_572/README.md) documents the 572-row degree table and the 1,836 selected finite-field
stratum records establishing continuous-convolution rigidity through degree 572, hence the counting formulas with
multiplicity for every `2 <= p <= 573`; this extends the length results, while distinctness has the separate scope above.

The small package contains exact generators, a coverage/record validator, and an msolve replay driver that removes its
temporary solver output; computed Gröbner bases and research scratch directories are not stored.
